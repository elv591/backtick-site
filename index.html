BACKTICK

Terminal widgets for your home and lock screen. Your calendar, reminders, weather and system stats as a live terminal — on-device, no account, no server.

Getting started

  • Open the app and grant what you want the widgets to show — calendar, reminders, location (for weather), health. Skip any; that panel just stays dark.
  • Long-press your home screen → tap Edit → Add Widget → search backtick.
  • Lock screen: long-press the clock → Customize.

Common questions

  • A widget says "grant access" — open the app and tap the row in the ACCESS panel. If you previously denied it, the row says settings > and takes you to iOS Settings.
  • Weather shows nothing — it needs location access and a moment for the first reading. Health showing 0? Check Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Backtick.
  • The tabs and buttons on widgets are tappable — the Terminal's tab bar switches panels, the Console's ▲▼⏎ navigate, tasks tick off with a tap, and yes, you can poke bit.
  • Restore Pro — the pro panel in the app has a restore link; purchases follow your Apple account.
